Business Insights — Superstore Sales Analytics

1. Furniture sub-categories (Tables, Bookcases) are the biggest profitability risk — 
   Tables alone lose ₹17,725 despite ₹206,965 in sales, suggesting excessive 
   discounting is eroding margins.

2. West region leads in both sales (₹725K) and profit (₹108K); South lags on both 
   metrics — a candidate for a regional strategy review.

3. Technology has the highest average profit per order (₹78.75), nearly 9x 
   Furniture's (₹8.70) — indicates strong margin potential if Technology's share 
   of sales is increased.

4. Home Office customers get the lowest average discount (14.7%) but generate the 
   highest average profit (₹33.82) — suggesting reduced discounting elsewhere could 
   improve profitability without hurting sales volume.

5. The discount-profit scatter plot shows a clear pattern: sub-categories with 
   discounts above ~30% tend to cluster near zero or negative profit — recommend 
   capping discounts on high-discount, low-margin categories.
